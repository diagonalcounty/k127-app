Statically linked inside libgit2.xcframework (https://github.com/light-tech/LibGit2-On-iOS/releases/download/v1.3.1/libgit2.xcframework.zip, checksum 332bfb255649f2295d5530e4abed4e81803acdc6abf18a266695fdb447ca3df2), which
