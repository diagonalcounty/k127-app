libssh2 1.10.0
Source: https://github.com/libssh2/libssh2 tag libssh2-1.10.0
Tarball named by the LibGit2-On-iOS v1.3.1 build script: libssh2-1.10.0.tar.gz

Statically linked inside libgit2.xcframework (https://github.com/light-tech/LibGit2-On-iOS/releases/download/v1.3.1/libgit2.xcframework.zip, checksum 332bfb255649f2295d5530e4abed4e81803acdc6abf18a266695fdb447ca3df2), which
MiniGit (https://github.com/diagonalcounty/MiniGit) revision bc14e1fdbe2eb24cf012a5776ff9759eb21bb174 downloads. The build sets
CRYPTO_BACKEND=OpenSSL and BUILD_SHARED_LIBS=NO, then libtool -static merges
the archive into libgit2.a. Headers/libssh2.h in that zip says
LIBSSH2_VERSION "1.10.0". Copyright lines in that shipped header: Sara
Golemon (2004-2009), Daniel Stenberg (2009-2015), Simon Josefsson (2010).
