zlib (system libz)
Dynamically linked. Not inside the xcframework.

MiniGit (https://github.com/diagonalcounty/MiniGit) revision bc14e1fdbe2eb24cf012a5776ff9759eb21bb174 sets .linkedLibrary("z") on both the XGit target and the MiniGit
target. The upstream README tells XGit-only clients to add libz.tbd.
libgit2 1.3.1 USE_BUNDLED_ZLIB defaults to OFF. The static archive has no
deflate.c.o, inflate.c.o, adler32.c.o, or crc32.c.o from libgit2's
deps/zlib. OpenSSL's libcrypto-lib-c_zlib.o is OpenSSL's wrapper, not a copy
of zlib.
