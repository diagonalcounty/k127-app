libiconv (system library)
Dynamically linked. Not vendored. Not inside the xcframework.

MiniGit (https://github.com/diagonalcounty/MiniGit) revision bc14e1fdbe2eb24cf012a5776ff9759eb21bb174 sets .linkedLibrary("iconv") on both the XGit target and the MiniGit target. The upstream
README tells XGit-only clients to add libiconv.tbd. ntlmclient's
unicode_iconv.c.o calls iconv. The package platforms are iOS 14 and macOS 11.
That does not pin an SDK drop of libiconv.

NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW. The license depends on which system libiconv the build
links. This notice does not pick a license and does not assert that either
drop below is the one K127 links, or that linking it is resolved.

Apple's published sources changed license:
- Tag libiconv-64 (imported 2022-09-30) has OpenSourceLicense LGPL,
  OpenSourceVersion 1.11, and OpenSourceURL
  http://ftp.gnu.org/pub/gnu/libiconv/libiconv-1.11.tar.gz, with COPYING.LIB
  in the tree.
  https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/libiconv/tree/libiconv-64
- Tag libiconv-99 (imported 2024-03-26) is a different tree. Its
  libiconv.txt states BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, and BSD-2-Clause-FreeBSD.
  https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/libiconv/blob/libiconv-99/libiconv.txt

Which of those a given app build links was not determined.
