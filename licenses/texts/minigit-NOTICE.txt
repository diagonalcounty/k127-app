Link: statically linked. Package.swift at the pinned revision declares the
XGit library without a dynamic product type. Its libgit2 dependency is a
binary target: an xcframework of static archives built by LibGit2-On-iOS
v1.3.1 (BUILD_SHARED_LIBS=NO, then libtool -static). The Xcode project links
XGit in the Frameworks phase and does not embed a MiniGit framework. System
zlib and libiconv are dynamic system libraries. At this revision Package.swift
sets .linkedLibrary("z") and .linkedLibrary("iconv") on both the XGit target and the MiniGit target.
K127 links the XGit product. See their notices.
